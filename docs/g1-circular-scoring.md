## 7.0 Scoring Methodology & Weightage

Each application receives marks across 6 categories. The total marks are calculated as a weighted sum:

| #  | Category | Weight |
|----|----------|--------|
| i  | Proximity (distance from school) | 50% |
| ii | School staff children | 25% |
| iii| Currently enrolled siblings at the school | 14% |
| iv | Alumni (past pupil) children | 6% |
| v  | Government employee children | 4% |
| vi | Special cases (disability, low income) | 1% |

### 7.1 Rules

- **7.1.1**: If allocated seats for a category are reduced, marks are proportionally adjusted. The reduced marks cannot be less than the minimum mark allocated for that category.
- **7.1.2**: If an applicant qualifies under multiple categories, only the highest scoring category is used (not cumulative).
- **7.1.3**: Distance-based scoring uses 1:10000 cadastral maps + Google Maps verification. District education offices maintain a distance appeals officer.
- **7.1.4**: Sibling/past pupil marks require verification that the sibling/alumni is currently attending the same school.
- **7.1.5**: Distance is measured from the permanent address on the application to the school. Each applicant's distance is recorded. If the applicant or parent/guardian disputes the distance, the school's distance officer must investigate and confirm within a reasonable period.
- **7.1.6**: Distance can be recalculated if errors or fraudulent claims are discovered.

## 7.2 Proximity Category — 50%

### 7.2.1 Distance Priority Score

The total 50% proximity score is split into sub-components. First, distance priority is calculated:

**Distance Band Scoring**:

| Distance | Score |
|----------|-------|
| < 0.5 km | 100% |
| 0.5 to < 1 km | 80% |
| 1 to < 2 km | 60% |
| 2 to < 3 km | 40% |
| 3 to < 5 km | 20% |
| 5 to < 6 km | 10% |
| >= 6 km | 5% |

**Distance is calculated from**: The applicant's permanent address (on the application form) to the school, using 1:10000 cadastral maps and verified via Google Maps.

**Required documents for distance scoring**:
- Certificate of residence
- 1931 Feb 19 ancestor land certificate (if available — gives significant priority)
- Additional documents if residing in the area for >10 years

### 7.2.1.1 Required Documents for Distance Priority

- Certificate of residence
- Death certificate (if applicable)
- NIC copy
- Birth certificate
- Government gazette (if parent/guardian was appointed/transferred before Feb 19, 1931 — gives significant priority)
- Land ownership documents (if applicable)
- Ancestor land certificate (if registered before 1931 Feb 19 — gives significant priority)
- Employment/transfer letter (if transferred to the area)

### Distance Priority Sub-Scoring (within 50%)

The distance component contributes up to **20 marks** (out of the 50%):

| Condition | Marks |
|-----------|-------|
| Applicant residing in the area for 5+ years | 20 |
| Applicant residing in the area for 3-4 years | 16 |
| Applicant residing in the area for 1-2 years | 10 |
| Government officer transferred to the area | Based on transfer letter |

### Duration of Residence Sub-Scoring (within 50%)

| Years of Continuous Residence | Marks |
|-------------------------------|-------|
| >= 5 years | 100% of sub-component |
| 4 to < 5 years | 80% |
| 3 to < 4 years | 60% |
| 2 to < 3 years | 40% |
| 1 to < 2 years | 20% |
| < 1 year | 10% |
| No proof | 5% |

### 7.2.2 Distance Priority Scoring for Admission (within 50%)

| Distance Band | Priority Points |
|---------------|----------------|
| < 0.5 km | 05 |
| 0.5 to < 1 km | 04 |
| 1 to < 2 km | 03 |
| 2 to < 3 km | 02 |
| 3 to < 5 km | 01 |
| >= 5 km | 00 |

### Parent/Guardian Birth Area Bonus (within 50%)

- If both parents were born in the area: **+5 marks**
- If grandparents were born in the area: **+3 marks**
- If applicant has an ancestor land certificate registered before 1931: **+5 marks**

### Government Transfer Bonus (within 50%)

- If parent/guardian is a government officer transferred to the area: **+5 marks** (verified via transfer letter)

### Combined Distance+Duration Score

**Total Proximity Score** = (Distance Priority Score + Duration Score + Parent Birth Bonus + Transfer Bonus) × 50% weight

## 7.3 School Staff Children Category — 25%

**Applies to**: Children of current school staff (teachers, administrative staff, workers) at the SAME school.

### 7.3.1 Base Staff Score

| Condition | Marks |
|-----------|-------|
| Parent is permanent staff at the same school | 25 |
| Parent is temporary/contract staff at the same school | 22.5 |
| Parent is permanent staff but at a different school | 20 |
| Parent is temporary/contract staff at a different school | 17.5 |
| Parent was staff but left within last 5 years | 15 |
| Parent was staff but left more than 5 years ago | 12.5 |

### 7.3.2 Staff Distance Adjustment

If the staff member lives more than **100 km** from the school, the staff score is reduced by **20%**.

### 7.3.3 Staff Employment Type Adjustment

- Permanent staff: Full score
- Temporary staff: Score × 0.5
- Contract staff: Score × 0.75

### 7.3.4 Staff Service Duration Bonus

| Years of Service at School | Bonus |
|---------------------------|-------|
| >= 10 years | +2 marks |
| 5 to < 10 years | +1 mark |
| < 5 years | +0 marks |

### Staff Documentation Requirements

- School staff appointment letter
- Service certificate
- NIC copy
- Birth certificate
- Government gazette (if appointed before 1931 Feb 19 — gives priority)
- Employment verification letter from school principal

---

## 7.4 Siblings Category — 14%

**Applies to**: Children who have siblings currently enrolled at the SAME school.

### 7.4.1 Sibling Verification Requirements

- Sibling must be currently attending the same school
- Sibling must be verified (enrollment confirmed)
- Both siblings must share at least one parent (same mother or same father)

### 7.4.2 Sibling Scoring Tiers

| # of Verified Siblings at School | Marks |
|----------------------------------|-------|
| 1 sibling | 14 |
| 2 siblings | 14 (capped) |
| 3+ siblings | 14 (capped) |

**Note**: The total is capped at 14 marks regardless of the number of siblings.

### 7.4.3 Sibling Age/Grade Priority

- Sibling in Grade 1-5: +2 marks
- Sibling in Grade 6-10: +1 mark
- Sibling in Grade 11+: +0 marks

### 7.4.4 Sibling Document Requirements

- Sibling's birth certificate
- Sibling's school enrollment verification
- NIC of sibling (if applicable)
- Proof of relationship (birth certificates showing same parent)

---

## 7.5 Alumni (Past Pupil) Category — 6%

**Applies to**: Children whose parent/guardian is a past pupil of the SAME school.

### 7.5.1 Alumni Verification

- Parent must have attended the same school
- Alumni status must be verified
- Highest grade completed is considered

### 7.5.2 Alumni Scoring Based on Highest Grade

| Highest Grade Completed | Score |
|------------------------|-------|
| GCE A/L or GCE O/L | 100% of 6% = 6.0 |
| Grade 11 | 80% of 6% = 4.8 |
| Grade 10 | 60% of 6% = 3.6 |
| Grade 9 | 40% of 6% = 2.4 |
| Grade 8 | 20% of 6% = 1.2 |
| Below Grade 8 | 10% of 6% = 0.6 |

### 7.5.3 Alumni Year-Left Priority

| Years Since Leaving School | Bonus |
|---------------------------|-------|
| <= 5 years | +0.5 marks |
| 6-10 years | +0.3 marks |
| > 10 years | +0.1 marks |

### Alumni Documentation Requirements

- Past pupil certificate from school
- NIC copy
- Birth certificate
- School enrollment records (if available)
- GCE O/L or A/L certificates (if applicable)

---

## 7.6 Government Employee Category — 4%

**Applies to**: Children whose parent/guardian is a government employee (මහ/මන්ත්‍රී/මන්ත්‍රිනී, etc.)

### 7.6.1 Government Employee Scoring

| Service Years | Score |
|---------------|-------|
| >= 20 years | 100% of 4% = 4.0 |
| 15 to < 20 years | 80% of 4% = 3.2 |
| 10 to < 15 years | 60% of 4% = 2.4 |
| 5 to < 10 years | 40% of 4% = 1.6 |
| < 5 years | 20% of 4% = 0.8 |

### 7.6.2 Government Transfer Priority

- If parent was transferred to the area within the last 5 years: **+1 mark**
- Transfer must be verified via official transfer letter

### Government Employee Documentation Requirements

- Government employee appointment letter
- Service certificate
- NIC copy
- Birth certificate
- Transfer letter (if applicable)
- Government gazette (if appointed before 1931 Feb 19)

## 7.7 Special Cases Category — 1%

**Applies to**: Children with disabilities, special needs, or from low-income families.

### 7.7.1 Disability/Special Needs Scoring

| Condition | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| Child has verified disability | 100% of 1% = 1.0 |
| Child has verified special needs | 100% of 1% = 1.0 |
| Parent has verified disability | 80% of 1% = 0.8 |

### 7.7.2 Low Income Scoring

| Income Level | Score |
|-------------|-------|
| Below Rs. 25,000/month | 100% of 1% = 1.0 |
| Rs. 25,000-50,000/month | 80% of 1% = 0.8 |
| Rs. 50,000-100,000/month | 60% of 1% = 0.6 |
| Rs. 100,000-200,000/month | 40% of 1% = 0.4 |
| Rs. 200,000-500,000/month | 20% of 1% = 0.2 |
| Above Rs. 500,000/month | 0% |

### 7.7.3 Armed Forces / Special Service Bonus

- Parent in armed forces: **+0.5 marks**
- Parent in special government service: **+0.3 marks**

### Special Cases Documentation Requirements

- Disability certificate (if applicable)
- Income certificate (for low income)
- Special needs assessment report
- Armed forces service letter (if applicable)

## 8.0 Application Scoring Process

### 8.1 Document Verification

Before scoring, all documents must be verified:
1. Birth certificate verified
2. NIC verified
3. Address verified
4. Category eligibility verified
5. Age eligibility verified

### 8.2 Age Eligibility

For 2027 admission:
- Child must be born between **2021.05.01** and **2022.04.30**
- Age verification is mandatory

### 8.3 Scoring Calculation

The scoring process:
1. Calculate each category score independently
2. Apply category weights (50%, 25%, 14%, 6%, 4%, 1%)
3. Sum all weighted scores
4. Total marks = sum of all category weighted scores
5. Total is clamped to [0.0, 100.0]

### 8.4 Marks Breakdown

Each application gets a `marks_breakdown` record per category with:
- `category_code`: PROX, STAFF, SIBLING, ALUMNI, GOVT, SPECIAL
- `raw_marks`: Raw score before weighting
- `max_raw_marks`: Maximum possible raw score (100)
- `weight_percentage`: Category weight (50, 25, 14, 6, 4, 1)
- `weighted_score`: (raw_marks / max_raw_marks) × weight_percentage
- `distance_km`: Distance to school (for PROX category)
- `distance_band`: Distance band (for PROX category)
- `calculation_rule`: Description of how score was calculated

## 9.0 Admission Lists

### 9.1 Main List

- Applicants who score above the cutoff threshold
- Ordered by total marks (highest first)
- Quota limits apply per category

### 9.2 Waiting List

- Applicants who qualify but exceed quota
- Ordered by total marks
- Maximum waiting list size = quota + 20

### 9.3 Rejected List

- Applicants who don't meet minimum criteria
- Or who scored below threshold

## 10.0 Appeals Process

### 10.1 Appeal Types

1. Distance calculation error
2. Document rejection
3. Category eligibility dispute
4. Scoring calculation error
5. Fraud allegation

### 10.2 Appeal Timeline

- Appeals must be filed within 7 days of list publication
- Decision within 14 days
- Further appeal to provincial education office within 30 days

## Summary: Complete Scoring Formula

```
Total Marks = 
    (Proximity Score × 0.50) +
    (Staff Score × 0.25) +
    (Sibling Score × 0.14) +
    (Alumni Score × 0.06) +
    (Govt Score × 0.04) +
    (Special Score × 0.01)
```

Where each category score is calculated independently based on the rules above.
